About GeoGym

GeoGym is a free geography game. Learn the flags, capitals, maps and shapes of the world, then race the clock to see how much you know.

What you can play

There are 47 quizzes: every country's flag, capital, outline and place on the map, plus currencies, languages, landmarks, lakes, rivers, mountains and seas. There are also quizzes on the states and provinces of the United States, Canada, Australia, Germany and Mexico. See all of them.

  • Practice brings back the ones you miss until you know them, and shows each answer on a map.
  • Quiz is a race against the clock, with no repeats. Your best score and time are saved.
  • Name them asks you to type every answer you can remember.
  • Browse is a reference list you can search.
  • The Daily Challenge has 10 questions, the same for everyone each day, so you can compare scores with friends.
  • Medals: bronze, silver and gold for learning half, most, or all of a quiz.
  • Multiplayer lets you race a friend live: make a game, send them the code, and you both answer the same questions at the same time.

Free, no sign-up

You don't need an account. Your progress is saved in your own browser, on your device. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Where the facts come from

Flags, borders and country facts come from open data projects: flag-icons, region-flags and Wikimedia Commons for flags; world-countries for capitals, currencies, languages and borders; Natural Earth, world-atlas and the US Census Bureau for maps, lakes, rivers and mountains; and the World Bank for population. Photos of capitals and landmarks are loaded from Wikipedia, and each one links to its article, where the photographer and license are listed. Landmarks, former names, peak heights and state nicknames were written for this game.

Borders follow international recognition, so Crimea is shown as part of Ukraine and Northern Cyprus as part of Cyprus.

Found a mistake?

Countries change their flags, capitals and names from time to time. If something looks wrong or out of date, please let us know.
